Construction Project Cost Analysis (Oracle PPM)

Merged 9 monthly Excel exports (Jan–Sep 2024) of construction cost transactions into one analyzable dataset: 9,017 entries across 159 suppliers, 118 cost items and 5 cost categories, with reversal/accrual analysis.

PythonExcelOracle PPMPDF ReportDashboard

The problem

Cost data lived in 9 separate monthly Oracle PPM exports with inconsistent structure — impossible to analyze as one picture.

What I did

  • Consolidated 9 exports into a single clean dataset of 9,017 cost transactions.
  • Structured the data by 159 suppliers, 118 cost-breakdown items and 5 cost categories (subcontractors the largest share at ~55%).

NLP — synthetic data

Arabic Support-Ticket Classifier

Classifies Arabic customer-support tickets into 9 categories and suggests the matching canned reply. Compared a rule-based classifier against a machine-learning baseline and reported the results honestly.

PythonNLPscikit-learnColabArabic

The problem

Support teams answer the same ticket types all day. If a first line can tag the ticket and suggest the right reply, agents answer faster and more consistently.

What I did

  • Created 90 synthetic Arabic tickets (10 per type) across 9 categories modeled on real e-commerce support patterns.
  • Built two classifiers in one Colab notebook: a transparent rule-based matcher and an ML baseline.
  • Evaluated both on a held-out split: rule-based 25/27 vs ML 18/27 — and documented why the ML model lagged on small data.

What it shows

Choosing the right tool for the data size, and reporting limits honestly — the split is small, so results are illustrative, not production accuracy.

Synthetic data only — no real customer tickets or company text.
